The win — the slowdown is holding across every window

The week is at one exit, July month-to-date is running at half of June over the same days, and the rolling 30 days is down 48% on the preceding 30. Three different lenses, same direction. Combined churn month-to-date is 3.9% — the first month all year tracking under the 5% KPI.

The concern — the leakage moved upstream

Counted churn was near zero, but the pipeline lost four prospects in the same seven days: a Kickstarter upfront marked NRO (no roll-over) after 28 days, and two teens whose one-week trial expired on 20 July without converting. None of these hit the churn number, but all of them are lost revenue. A quiet cancellation week can hide a leaky front door.

The context — next week is already at three

Three counted cancellations are already lodged for 24–30 July (Jaxon Gibbs, Bryan Hinkins, Bay Pedersen) and one for the following week (Josh Simpson, "can't afford it"). Treat this week as a genuinely good week, not as a new baseline.

Three teens said they'd stopped enjoying it

Diego Di Donato, Angus Gillespie and Beau Dun all left in this window with a variant of "not interested anymore" — after 144, 226 and 308 days. The 6-month wall for adults

The 181–210 day band is the single biggest spike in the data — 27 exits, 20 of them adults. Median adult tenure is 182 days and the Accelerate minimum term is 182 days. Adults aren't drifting away randomly; they're reaching the end of the contract and stopping. Trey Jurd left at 158 days, three weeks short of full term. The renewal conversation is currently happening at month six, which is after the decision. It needs to happen at month four.

The term cliff for under-18s

The 91–120 day band holds 13 under-18 exits — the highest U18 bucket in the year — and 35% of all U18s are gone within 90 days. That's roughly one school term. Teens on the unlimited product hold 70 days longer than teens on 2x classes, so the step-up conversation matters. The intervention point is week 10 to 12, before the term ends and the habit breaks over the break.
