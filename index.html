The win — July is running at half of June

Month to date (1–23 July) sits at 12 counted exits against 24 over the same days in June. Adults are down 37%, U18 down 75%. Combined churn month-to-date is 3.9% — the first month all year tracking under the 5% KPI. Whatever changed after the June exodus is holding.

The concern — the leakage moved upstream

Counted churn was near zero, but the pipeline lost four prospects in the same seven days: a Kickstarter upfront marked NRO (no roll-over) after 28 days, and two teens whose one-week trial expired on 20 July without converting. None of these hit the churn number, but all of them are lost revenue. A quiet cancellation week can hide a leaky front door.

The context — next week is already at three

Three counted cancellations are already lodged for 24–30 July (Jaxon Gibbs, Bryan Hinkins, Bay Pedersen) and one for the following week (Josh Simpson, "can't afford it"). Treat this week as a genuinely good week, not as a new baseline.

Nearly a third of exits have no usable reason

60 of 199 counted exits this year carry a reason like "Not using", "Didnt specify why", "Oh no forgot to cancel eek" or a blank dash. That's the single largest category and it's a process problem, not a member problem. Until the cancellation form forces a category, every retention decision is being made on two-thirds of the picture.

Relocation is genuinely the biggest real reason

Once the unknowns are set aside, relocation is the largest identifiable category — 32 exits year to date, and every single one an adult. It's unavoidable churn, and it means the true preventable churn rate is meaningfully lower than the headline. Worth remembering before reading the monthly numbers as a failure.

The 15-week teen window

Half of all teen and kid exits happen inside 105–116 days — about 15 weeks, or a single school term. Teens on the unlimited product hold 70 days longer than teens on 2x classes. The intervention point is week 10 to 12: a coach conversation, a grading goal, or a step up to unlimited, before the term ends and the habit breaks over the holidays.

The 6-month wall for adults

Median tenure on the Accelerate weekly 6-month membership is 172 days — the term is 182. Adults aren't drifting away randomly; they're reaching the end of the minimum term and stopping. Trey Jurd left at 158 days, three weeks short. The renewal conversation is currently happening at month six, which is too late. It needs to happen at month four, while they're still training.
